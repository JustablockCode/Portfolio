About

I created an improved timelapse preset for the Bambu Lab A1 Mini.

The problem with standard timelapses: Standard presets require the toolhead to move to the wiper for every single shot. This wastes time and degrades print quality by leaving bits of extruded filament on your walls at every layer change.

How this preset works: This custom configuration triggers the camera snapshot without moving the toolhead at all, saving time and keeping your outer walls clean.

TIP FOR BEST VIDEO: You must turn on the printer's LED light manually from app or the printer screen, otherwise the resulting timelapse model will be too dark and look bad.
